# v1.37.4 — OpenRouter webhook receiver: fast reply and no duplicate spans

Released: 2026-11-16

Two problems in `evals/openrouter_webhook_receiver.py`, found on the first real
deliveries after a Berserk outage.

## What was wrong

**Most spans arrived twice.** Of 12 distinct spans delivered in one test
session, 9 were delivered two times, 2 to 9 seconds apart. The receiver
waited for Berserk's ingest before it answered OpenRouter. Ingest takes about
two seconds to answer, plus the time to redact long prompts, so OpenRouter
treated the delivery as slow and sent it again. The retried copy was recorded
and forwarded a second time, which double-counts tokens and cost in Berserk.

**A full disk dropped the connection.** When the capture file could not be
written, the handler raised, the connection closed without a reply, and
nothing told OpenRouter to try again.

## What changed

- **The reply goes out right after the local capture is written.** Redaction
  and the forward to Berserk run on two background workers behind a bounded
  queue (200 deliveries). The response now says `"berserk_forward": "queued"`,
  `"skipped"` (nothing new to forward) or `"dropped"` (queue full), instead of
  `"berserk_forwarded": true|false`. OpenRouter ignores the field. A dropped or
  failed forward loses nothing: the delivery is in the local capture, and the
  backfill script can send it later. A full queue is logged through the same
  `FAILING` / `RECOVERED` lines as other forward failures.
- **Each `(traceId, spanId)` is handled once.** A retried delivery stays in the
  raw capture (which records every delivery) but is not written to the spans
  file or forwarded again. The response reports `"duplicates_skipped"`. The set
  is bounded (5,000 pairs, oldest evicted). A span without both ids is never
  treated as a duplicate.
- **The claim happens before the write and is released if the write fails.**
  Two simultaneous copies of one span cannot both pass, and a retry after a
  real failure is processed, not skipped.
- **A storage failure answers `500 {"ok": false, "error": "storage failure"}`**
  so OpenRouter retries, and logs the cause.
- A payload that is valid JSON but not an object, or has an unexpected shape,
  is kept in the raw capture and answered `200 {"parsed": false}` instead of
  crashing the handler.

## Verification

Eleven new tests, including: the reply returns in under a second while a
forward is still blocked; a duplicate delivery is recorded once and forwarded
once while the raw capture keeps both; overlapping payloads forward only the
new spans; a full queue still replies and records; a failed write gives 500
and the retry is processed. The existing forwarding tests were adapted to the
background worker (they now wait for the queue to drain and use distinct span
ids).

Each behaviour was also broken on purpose in a scratch run to confirm the
tests fail: dedupe off, claim not released, reply waiting for the forward,
full queue never dropping, and the span-file and raw-capture write errors not
caught. All six mutations were caught.
